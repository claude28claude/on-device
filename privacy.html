Privacy

The short version: we collect nothing. There is no we. There is no server.

What we collect

Nothing.

That is not a turn of phrase. There is no computer anywhere that receives anything from this site. It is a folder of files served to your browser, and after that everything happens on your machine.

What about my files?

They stay on your device. They are opened by your browser, worked on by your browser, and saved by your browser. They are never transmitted. You can confirm this by switching off your internet and using the site anyway — it works, which would be impossible if your files were going somewhere.

Cookies

None. Not "essential cookies", not "we use cookies to improve your experience". None at all, which is why you have not seen a banner.

Analytics and tracking

None. No page views, no events, no error reporting, no session recording, no A/B tests and no advertising. Nothing on this site is loaded from anyone else’s computer — not fonts, not icons, not code. Two open-source libraries are used for the PDF tools, but they are stored inside this site and served from this address like everything else, and the security policy stops them contacting anything just as it stops our own code doing so.

What is stored on your device

Your settings and pinned tools, and — only if you leave that switch on — the files you have loaded and the results you have produced, so that a refresh does not lose your work. All of it sits in your own browser's storage. None of it is readable by us, because there is no us to read it.

The full list, and a button that erases every bit of it.

Who can see what you do here

Whoever hosts these files can see that a browser at your IP address requested them, in the same way any website is requested. They cannot see which tool you used or which file you opened, because that information never leaves your machine. If even that bothers you, download the site and open it from your own disk.

Children

There is nothing here to sign up to, nothing to buy, and no data collected from anybody of any age.

Changes

If this ever changes, it changes in the public source code, where anyone can see it. There is no version of this site that quietly starts collecting things; the browser's own security policy would have to be weakened first, and that is written in plain sight at the top of every page.

Contact

There is no contact form, because a form would send something somewhere. If you find a problem, raise it wherever the source code is published.
