Layout

Default view How the tool list is shown when you arrive.
Pin tools from the homepage by pressing the pin on a tool's card.
Sidebar on the homepage A column down the side with the categories, your pinned tools and your recipes. On a narrow screen it sits above the tools instead.

Your tool list

Rename anything to whatever you actually call it, move things up and down, and hide the ones you never use. A hidden tool disappears from the homepage and from the search box, and its page still works if you have it bookmarked.

Behaviour and defaults

Download results automatically Off means finished files wait in the results tray.
Keep loaded files after a refresh Files are stored on this device only, in the browser's own database.
Clear loaded files when the tab closes Applied when you next open the site, because a browser cannot be trusted to finish work as it is closing. We would rather say that than pretend.
Keep a local history of what was processed Off by default. Stored on this device only, never sent anywhere.
Ask before anything destructive Clearing files, wiping settings, and anything that cannot be undone.
