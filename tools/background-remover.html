Remove a background

Cut a subject out of a picture and save it with a see-through background — or drop it onto a colour of your choosing.

Read this first — it decides whether this tool is any use to you

This works by colour. You point at the background, and every pixel close enough to that colour becomes see-through. It does not know what a person or a dog or a chair looks like.

So: a product on a white sweep, a logo, a signature on paper, a passport photo — excellent. A person against a busy street — it cannot, and it will say so when you load the picture rather than handing you a mess. Hair and fur it will cut straight through, because colour cannot tell a strand of hair from the wall behind it. There is a brush for fixing the awkward parts by hand.

Options

Which pixels to remove

Spreads inwards from the edges, so an enclosed area of the same colour — the hole in a letter O, a gap under an arm — is kept.

12 out of 60. Higher removes more shades of the background, and eventually starts eating the subject.

1 pixel.

Pulls the edge in by 1 pixel, which removes the halo of old background colour that otherwise clings to the subject.

Background colours you have pointed at

Click the picture to add one. With none, the colour is guessed from the edges.

Brush

Turn the brush on, then drag on the picture. Erase makes things see-through; Restore brings them back.

What to put behind the subject

See-through backgrounds are saved as PNG, because JPEG cannot store them.

Finished files land in the results tray at the bottom of the screen.
