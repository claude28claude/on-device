Checksum a file

Work out a file's fingerprint, so you can check a download matches what the publisher said it should be.

What this tool cannot do

MD5 and SHA-1 are both broken for proving a file is genuine: two different files can be made to share one. They are still fine for spotting a corrupted download, and are offered for that reason with the warning attached.

Options

Paste the checksum the publisher listed and we will say whether it matches.

Finished files land in the results tray at the bottom of the screen.
