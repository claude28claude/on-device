Blur or pixelate

Hide a face, a number plate, an address, or a name in a screenshot, for good.

What this tool cannot do

A light blur can sometimes be undone by software, and fine pixelation over text has been reversed before. A solid block never can. This tool tells you which side of that line your settings fall on.

A solid block cannot be undone by anyone.
