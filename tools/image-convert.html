Convert images

Change a picture from one format to another — including out of the HEIC format iPhones use, which little else will open.

What this tool cannot do

It cannot save HEIC, only read it — and reading HEIC depends entirely on your browser. Safari usually manages; Chrome, Edge and Firefox usually do not. On Device does not yet bundle its own HEIC decoder, and says so rather than failing quietly.
