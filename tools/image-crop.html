Crop images

Trim a picture by hand, to a fixed shape, or to the exact measurements an official photo needs.

What this tool cannot do

Official photo sizes are set by each country and do change. The presets here are the common published measurements, and the tool produces the right pixel size at the stated resolution — but it cannot judge whether your face is positioned acceptably. Check the requirements before you rely on it.
