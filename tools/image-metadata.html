What your photo is hiding

Every photo carries more than the picture. See all of it, then remove it — without re-saving the image, so nothing is lost.

What this tool cannot do

It cannot yet read the hidden information inside HEIC photos, so it will not tell you a HEIC has no location — it will tell you it cannot check. JPEG, PNG, WebP and TIFF are read fully.

Options

Removed without re-saving

The hidden sections are cut out of the file and the picture data is left exactly as it was. Your photo is not re-compressed, so no quality is lost.

Finished files land in the results tray at the bottom of the screen.
