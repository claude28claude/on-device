Images to PDF

Put pictures into a PDF: a scanned form, a set of receipts, photographs of a document.

What this tool cannot do

A PDF can only carry JPEG and PNG pictures directly. Anything else, including iPhone HEIC photos, needs converting first, which the Convert tool does on this same device.

Options

72 points is one inch.

Finished files land in the results tray at the bottom of the screen.
