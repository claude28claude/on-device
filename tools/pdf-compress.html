Compress a PDF

Make a PDF smaller — and understand exactly what you give up, before you give it up.

What this tool cannot do

There is no way to make a PDF dramatically smaller without changing it. The tidy method is completely safe but usually saves little. The flatten method saves a great deal but turns text into pictures, so it can no longer be selected, searched or copied. Both are offered here with their real numbers; neither is disguised.
