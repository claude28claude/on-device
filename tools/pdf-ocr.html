Read a scanned document

Work out what the words say in a scan or a photograph, so you can search and copy them.

What this tool cannot do

This needs a large one-time download of about 17 MB, because the recognition engine and its English language data are stored here rather than fetched from someone else's server. English only for now. It reads printed text well and handwriting badly.

Options

Only applies to PDFs. Pictures are read as they are.

Recognition is slow — a few seconds a page. Start with one page.

What works and what does not

Clear printed text on a straight, well-lit page reads well. Handwriting, heavy backgrounds, faint photocopies and photographs taken at an angle read badly. The tool tells you how confident it is rather than presenting a guess as a transcript.

Finished files land in the results tray at the bottom of the screen.
