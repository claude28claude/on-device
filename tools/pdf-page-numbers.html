Page numbers and headers

Number the pages, and add a line of your own along the top or bottom.

What this tool cannot do

It uses the standard typefaces every PDF reader already has, so nothing is downloaded. That means Helvetica, Times and Courier only.

Options

Use {n} for the number and {total} for how many there are. For example: Page {n} of {total}

Goes on the opposite edge to the page number.

11 points.

Finished files land in the results tray at the bottom of the screen.
