Redact — properly

Black out what should not be seen, and actually destroy it. Not a rectangle drawn on top of readable words.

What this tool cannot do

A redacted page becomes a picture, so its text can no longer be selected or searched. That is not a side effect, it is how the content is destroyed. Pages you did not mark are left exactly as they were.

Options

Why other tools get this wrong

Most tools draw a black rectangle over the words. The words are still in the file — anyone can select the text underneath and copy it out. Newspapers and governments have published documents redacted that way and had the hidden text extracted within hours. This tool throws the original page away and rebuilds it from the blacked-out picture.

Marked areas

Drag across the page to mark something.

Only pages you marked are rebuilt at this quality.

After redacting, the finished file is read back and searched for this text. You should not have to take our word for it.

Finished files land in the results tray at the bottom of the screen.
