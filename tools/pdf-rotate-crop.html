Rotate and crop pages

Turn pages that came out sideways, and trim margins off the edges.

What this tool cannot do

Cropping a PDF hides the trimmed edges rather than destroying them. The content is still inside the file and determined software could recover it. For anything sensitive use Redact a PDF, which genuinely destroys what it covers.
