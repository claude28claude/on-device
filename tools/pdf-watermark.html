Watermark a PDF

Stamp a word or a logo across the pages, at whatever strength you choose.

What this tool cannot do

A watermark drawn on top can be removed by anyone with the right software. It marks a document; it does not secure one. For that, use a password.

Options

60 points.

25% — faint enough to read through.

45 degrees.

Finished files land in the results tray at the bottom of the screen.
