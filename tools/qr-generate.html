Make a QR code

For a link, your wifi details, a contact card, an email, or any text at all.

What this tool cannot do

It makes QR codes; it does not read them. The reader is not built, and the reason is on the roadmap. Put a logo in the middle only at a high error-correction level, and always test the result by scanning it.

Options

Scanners need white space around a code. Four is the standard.
